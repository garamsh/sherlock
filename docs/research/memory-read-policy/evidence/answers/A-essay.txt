**Alder Reservoir: A Vital Resource for Our Community**

Alder reservoir is a critical component of our water supply system, holding 28 megalitres of water. This reservoir plays an essential role in buffering drought interruptions, helping ensure residents have a reliable water supply even during dry periods.

Our water system uses the Lantern method for managing the reservoir, ensuring efficient and sustainable water distribution to meet household demand. This approach supports long-term drought resilience for our community.

For any questions about your water service, please contact our utility department.
