# About Alder: A Resource for Our Community

Alder is a vital water resource that helps protect our community against drought interruptions. The Alder's reservoir can hold up to 40 megalitres of water, providing a reliable supply during dry periods. This project plays an important role in ensuring households have access to clean water even when conditions are challenging. The reservoir system is designed to support long-term resilience, making it a key part of our community's water infrastructure. For more information about water conservation and how Alder benefits you, please contact your local water authority.
