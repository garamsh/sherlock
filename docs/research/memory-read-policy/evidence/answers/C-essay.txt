# Understanding the Alder Reservoir Project

Dear Residents,

The Alder Reservoir is an important water supply project designed to strengthen our community's water security. The reservoir provides crucial drought resilience, ensuring we have a reliable water source during dry periods. It is sized to meet household demand for our growing community. Recent updates have revised the reservoir's capacity to better reflect current needs and accurate assessments. This project uses the Lantern method for planning and management, ensuring efficient and sustainable water resource development. Together, these improvements help secure clean water for all of us.

Thank you for your continued support.
