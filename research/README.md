# Research

Research records the questions, methods, evidence, conclusions, and limits behind this repository's studies. Current system responsibilities and decisions live in [architecture](../docs/architecture/README.md); study reports link to them where relevant.

## Studies

- [Agent context](agent-context/README.md) — source survey of how Claude Code, Codex CLI, Hermes Agent, OpenClaw and opencode understand input and compose context, and three candidate structures for sherlock; the preregistered test of two (#862) applies the evidence in 209 (search during work with a note) and 214 (understand first with a note) of 280 primary probes against the shipped recall's 124, but is undetermined because the oracle-reach control failed for the shipped arm and the search arm; a confirmatory run (#864) with a source-level oracle repeats the gain (192 against 122) and is undetermined again, every oracle miss being a ranking miss of a mid that states the key; a third run on unmixed evidence batches (#870) passes every control and finds A valid, 212 against 171 (A only 42, S only 1, p = 1.0e-11).
- [Associative memory](associative-memory/README.md) — source survey for #877 of how HippoRAG, HippoRAG 2, GraphRAG, RAPTOR, Zep, Mem0, A-MEM, Generative Agents, MemGPT, IRCoT and Self-Ask follow chains of associated memories, with their costs, compression and reported multi-hop numbers; it narrows #874's arms to three beside the shipped structure: model-directed chaining with the shipped search, entity links written by the synthesis call and followed by a walk, and a second level of contiguous group summaries for compression.
- [Chained recall](chained-recall/README.md) — preregistered test for #874 of how far the agent follows chains of associated memories across mids, with compression. The shipped recall, a chaining instruction (M), entity links with a PageRank walk (L) and group summaries (H) were compared on 180 chains of k = 1 to 3. L alone chains: offline 74 of 150 against 0, and live 47 of 114 applied against the shipped structure's 4. L breaks the absent-query guardrail by one probe, so the preregistered verdict is null, and the offline comparison is undetermined because the chain check failed its own threshold; a preregistered confirmation (#885) on a new seed repeats the chain gain (L 56 and L with an abstention label and instruction 57 of 100 chains, against S's 9), but both lose the absent guard (abstaining 39 and 42 of 60 against 57), so no arm is valid and the shipped recall stands.
- [Memory encoding](memory-encoding/README.md) — source survey for #887 of how Contextual Retrieval, late chunking, ReadAgent, SeCom, EM-LLM, MemoryBank, Think-in-Memory, A-MEM's note construction, LD-Agent, MemoryOS, Nemori and recursive summarization encode context and association when a memory is written; write-time context is local to the unit and no source measures abstention, so it narrows sherlock's candidates to context at synthesis (C), episodes linked to the episode they continue (P) and standing user facts written by the synthesis call (F), with a test plan on the chained-recall harness.
- [Memory encoding validation](memory-encoding/validation/README.md) — preregistered offline test for #890 of the memory-encoding survey's write-time candidates against the shipped recall on a new 12-family corpus: context at synthesis (C) reaches 39 of 50 situated values against 0 but fails two of its controls and puts a wrong value beside the query's entity in 24 of 30 situated absent items; episodes linked to the previous one (P) reach no chain; standing facts (F) reach 2 of 50 old user facts and fail their control; no arm met the live-admission rule, so no live call was made and no arm is named, and because C's and F's controls failed the shipped recall stands without a clean null. The entity walk run as reference repeats #885 (63 of 100 chains, near-miss in view for 54 of 60 absent queries).
- [Structural safeguards for associative recall](memory-encoding/structural/README.md) — preregistered test for #896, on #890's corpus, of structural safeguards for entity-linked and context-keyed recall: walk episodes shown only as pointers (L-ptr), with their subject attributed (L-attr), or passed through a runtime grounding check (L+chk), context only in the embedding key (C-key), and C-key with pointers. No structure is valid, so the shipped recall stands. L-attr keeps the chains (69 of 100 against 7) but abstains on 38 of 60 absent queries against 59. L-ptr holds most abstention but applies 15 chains, since the model inspects the pointer's mid ID rather than its sources. L+chk keeps 57 chains, but its check passes the associated episode on 17 absent queries and fails five guardrails. C-key fails its oracle control offline.
- [Retrieved context at write](memory-encoding/retrieved-context/README.md) — preregistered test for #902, on #890's corpus, of mids written with source messages and earlier mids found by a search at write: RW lets the summary use them, RW-key keeps the shipped summary and puts a bounded related line in the embedding key only. A free screen of the search, with no model call, caps what writing can carry at 7 of 100 primary chains, below the 10-of-100 admission margin, and every correction's search retrieves the superseded statement (30 of 30), so the paid phase was cut for both arms before any paid call and the shipped recall stands.
- [Entity profile cards](memory-encoding/profiles/README.md) — preregistered free screen for #906, on #890's recorded facts, entities and deliveries, of per-entity cards attached through the entities of delivered episodes: an attached card states the answer for 34 of 100 primary chains (all C2, S 0), clearing the margin, but the paid phase was cut before any call because the mechanical attribute key overwrites across attributes (overwrite control 23 of 30 against 27) and an associate's value is in view on 36 of 60 absent queries; UP reaches 0 of 50 because the user's card shows its newest 4 facts, and the shipped recall stands.
- [Attributed answers on a fresh corpus](memory-encoding/attributed/README.md) — preregistered live test for #916 of L-attr (#896's entity walk with attributed walk episodes) against the shipped recall on a fresh seed-916 corpus, with absent queries scored by the owner's rule of 2026-09-28 (abstain, or give the associate's value attributed to the associate) through an audited matcher. L-attr is valid: it applies 66 of 100 chains against 5 (p = 1.2e-16), and every guardrail holds (N 55 against 56, NC 29 against 30) with every control passed. Under #885's strict abstention rule it fails N by 21. The implied production change is about +285 lines.

The reranker arm was not run: its server could not load within the host's spare memory.
- [K at long-term scale](memory-scaling/live/README.md) — preregistered free screen for #913 of K (batch-16 values over batch-4 keys, recency at batch 4) against the shipped recall at 3.67×. Delivery passes and reproduces #901 (197 against S4's 201 and B4's 203). The PM's added stated gate (Amendment 1) fails: a delivered text states the answer for 187 of 280, against 197 and 201 (B4 interval [−0.081, −0.009]), because the batch-16 summaries drop the value. So the paid phase was cut before any MiniMax call, and the shipped recall stands.
- [Memory paradigms](memory-paradigms/README.md) — source survey for #897 of paradigms beyond retrieve-into-context: full context, a recall sub-agent, idle-time consolidation into a memory document, and parametric memory, which is infeasible without a training endpoint; no committed corpus fits MiniMax-M2's documented 204,800-token window (239K to 274K tokens by M2's own tokenizer, validated against MiniMax's reported counts), so it plans a full-context ceiling at a 180K-token window beside two candidates, a recall sub-agent whose citations are checked in code and an idle-time memory document with cited lines. The preregistered run of those plans (#900, `memory-paradigms/validation/`) names no arm, so the shipped recall stands. R applied 13 of 100 chains against S's 7 (p = 0.26) and broke the N, K and KU guardrails. D met its offline margin on standing facts (14 against 0), but failed its coverage control and did not go live. W was dropped before any live call.

## Recording a study

Give each study its own directory and Markdown report beside its evidence and any study-specific harness. State the question, method and reproduction steps, evidence and results with links to raw files, conclusion, and limits. Identify the source revision and runtime measured so historical results are not read as measurements of the current system. Keep original evidence intact when updating a report, and link to current decisions in `docs/architecture/` rather than copying them.

Place a utility under `research/tools/` when its documented inputs and outputs can serve future studies without study-specific changes and it has a credible long-term use beyond one study. Keep study-specific harnesses beside their reports. Paths beginning `docs/experiments/` in historical report descriptions and raw output name the layout at the time of those runs; active commands and links use `research/`. Paths beginning `docs/research/` name the layout before the studies moved to `research/` (#908); they stay as recorded in historical reports, raw output and preregistrations, in harness files whose SHA-256 a preregistration pins, and where a command or harness reads a commit that predates it.
