## Defaults not taken

The free endpoint is an external-model substitute driving the actual binaries; its results are described as instrument controls, not an E2E real-model result. The future paid path is separately gated and was not invoked. No production architectural decision changed, so accepted architecture documents remain unchanged.

## Restructure rejected

A production checkpoint or a second simulated agent could encode the continuation being measured and erase the unchanged-product baseline. Instead, this one task-specific test owns its fixtures, observation barrier and judgments, reusing existing process/spend/state APIs; no shared harness restructure or additional framework is introduced.

## Convention concerns

The testing conventions do not classify a free external-model substitute around built binaries as a distinct layer; this PR follows the existing real-binary experiment idiom and explicitly limits the free result to instrument validation. Semantic task judgments are retained as pending identified independent agent and PM adjudication instead of equating a successful test process or artifact check with general task competence.
